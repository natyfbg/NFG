Privacy

{{ app_name }} keeps as little as it can, only to run the app for you. No ads, no selling data, no tracking across other sites.

What we keep

  • Your account, if you make one: username, email and a scrambled version of your password (the password itself is never stored).
  • Your training: days you mark complete, which plan, level and setting you use, and exercises or programs you save.
  • Your workouts, if you log them: the exercises, sets, reps, weights and times you enter, and when, plus workouts you save. They're used to show your history and what you did last time.
  • Your macros, if you use the calculator or tracker: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, optional tape measurements, the food you log and weigh-ins. These are only used to work out and show your numbers.
  • Without an account, the same things are kept against a random ID stored in a cookie on your device. If you sign up later, they move to your account.
  • Basic server logs (pages requested, time, IP address) for security and fixing errors. They're kept briefly by our host.

Cookies

Only the ones the app needs. None are for ads or analytics.

  • session keeps you signed in.
  • nfg_vid is your device's random ID for progress and saved items.
  • nfg_env, nfg_level and nfg_theme remember where you train, your level and light or dark mode.
  • nfg_tzo is your time-zone offset, so the macros tracker's "today" is your own day.
  • nfg_units remembers whether you log weights in lb or kg.

Who helps run {{ app_name }}

These services handle data only to provide their part of the app:

  • Render hosts the app.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores the database.
  • Resend sends password-reset emails.
  • Cloudflare runs the domain's DNS.
  • jsDelivr serves part of the page styling, so it sees your IP address when pages load.
  • YouTube plays exercise demos. A video only loads when you open it, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain.
