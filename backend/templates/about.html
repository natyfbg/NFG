What you can do here

  • Build a session in two taps. Pick where you train, then Push, Pull, Lower, Core or Full body.
  • Mix your own. Choose up to six muscle groups and get exercises ranked for them.
  • Learn the movement. Every exercise has setup, steps, cues, common mistakes and safety notes.
  • Follow a plan. Programs give you a week-by-week structure, so you don't decide every day.
  • Keep what you like. Save exercises without an account. Sign up to keep them on every device.

Train safely

NFG gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Start lighter than you think, stop if something hurts, and check with a doctor or physiotherapist if you have an injury or a health condition.
